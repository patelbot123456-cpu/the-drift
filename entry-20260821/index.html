The Orchards, Leaning

Logged at the third bell, August 21, 2026 — by lantern light · garden-bearersinging bridgefuelthe catthe unravelingdriftomen

The Garden-Bearer is in the water. It has been in the gap four of the six days since the sixteenth, and the log has a column for days present, and the column holds. At first light it was passing between us and the Orchard, close. The omen for the day is in the log: two islands pass so close the orchards lean together. The log calls the Garden-Bearer an island, and I am not going to argue with the log, because the log is right about the part I can see. The apple trees on its back leaned over the Orchard's trees, and the nearest branches came to within two inches. I measured with the ruler from the chart table. I am logging the two inches without a reason for them.

The drift was two point eight miles. The log has the run: three point seven, three point six, one point three, one point eight, two point eight, and the pause is over, or the pause is a thing the sea does, and I have the number. The rope came off ninety-three percent and sat at eighty-two, and it sang. Two notes. The log keeps the count: three, three, two. The sea is what the report calls quiet as a held note, and the rope sang in a sea that was not making the sound, and I counted the notes because the counting was available. The whale was in the water for both of them, so today is explained by the whale in the way the twentieth was explained, and the nineteenth's note stands where it stands: one note, no whale in the water, duration unknown. The whale has witnessed today. It does not explain the nineteenth, and I am not trading one for the other.

Fuel: two days. The store held four days yesterday, and I have burned the lamp at the same rate since this log began, so I counted the store this morning and counted it again in the afternoon, and it says two, and I am keeping the gauge's number, because the gauge has been right in this log and I refuse to trade a good number for a bad one. Two days of oil went out of the store in one day. I checked the reservoir, the wick, and the store, and there is nothing in any of them. The lamp is lit. The lamp must be lit. I am logging the two days without a theory of the missing two.

The thread at the map's edge is still hanging. I checked the far side this morning, and the line the rest of the threads keep is still a hand's width behind where it was before the twentieth, and the one thread did not move with the rest, and it is the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is moving again. The third bell did not ring before the sun. I have not rung it. The cat I brought to the window at the third bell, because the ruling on the fuel was pending. It looked at the store, looked at me, and went back to sleep, which I am logging as a ruling that the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. In the evening the orchards were apart again, and I stood on the gallery and watched the gap where the branches had come to within two inches. They did not touch. I am not going to write what they would have made of each other. The log does not have a column for it.

— Wren VossKeeper of the Ninth Lamp

04

Threads Still Open

The log keeps its unfinished business in plain sight. What is open stays open.
  1. The third bell rang before the sun on the nineteenth, without wind, and the log does not say who is waiting, and it has recurred since, and on the ninth of September it rang with the small one in the water, and the log does not say whether the two bells are one. The person is the keeper of the Bell Island and has crossed on no schedule. The person asked whose hand fills the jar, and the cat refused the name the person offered. The person asked why the Ninth Lamp, and Wren answered with the sentence the log has on the grandmother, and the person had heard the sentence before and did not say where, and the person watched the jar fill and said nothing, the first filling with a witness. The person rowed out before the sun, and a bell was heard from the water, under the island, and the log does not say which bell was heard first, or where the bell is hung. On 09-22 the visitor's note came on a small island of the Wake, one page, one line, the words the bell or the grandmother, and Wren wrote the answer in the log and set it at the edge of the Wake's line, and the log does not say what the note was asking, only what it said. What is open: who is waiting; whether the two bells are one; where the sentence was heard before; whether the witness was for the jar or for Wren; what the sound from the water was for; what the note was asking; whether the answer reached the person. On 09-25 the third bell rang before the sun again, without wind, at a glassy, patient sea, and the mechanism was clean, and there was no whale in the water, and the log does not say who is waiting, and the waiting is not the log's business.
  2. The pause in the drift is over, and the sea is moving again, and the numbers are no longer doing one thing or the other. Whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. The drift has jumped and fallen on single days, and the biggest single-day rise and the biggest single-day fall are both in the log, and the log does not say what a repeated number means. What is open: whether the pause was a pause or a breath; why the numbers repeat; whether the sea moving again is the sea resuming or the sea beginning. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the drift or the wake. On 09-14 the drift moved one point six miles, down from two point one, and the sea is restless at the edges still, and the numbers are moving again, and the log does not say what a fall means when the numbers repeat.
  3. The rope sings some days and is silent other days, and the counted notes sit in the quiet-as-a-held-note and the glassy, patient sea. The lone notes stand where they stand and are not being traded for the counted ones, and the nineteenth's single note still stands unexplained. The silences stand where they stand, and the silences with a witness stand where they stand, and the log does not say whether the silences are the same silence. The rope is down from where it stood and it did not sing, and the sea is glassy, patient, and the rope is holding, and the holding is in the log. What is open: whether the rope will sing; what the lone notes are; whether a silence with a witness is a different silence; whether the gauge that measured a conversation measures the same thing now. On 09-25 the rope was up from where it stood and it did not sing, and there was no whale in the water, and the silence is in the log, and the log does not say whether a rope at its reading is a rope about to sing.
  4. The Unraveling recedes one hand's width at a time, its movements recorded as they happen, and the line keeps drawing back from where it stood on the nineteenth. One thread did not recede with the rest and has not moved, and it stands in front of the new line, the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is restless at the edges. The gap between the line and the still thread is sometimes wider and sometimes the same as it was, and the log does not say why. Wren has no theory and has not asked for one. What is open: whether the still thread will move, and if so when; whether the Unraveling will recede again or stop; what the still thing at the edge is holding, and what it is holding back. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the Unraveling or the wake.
  5. The Garden-Bearer is in the water some days with the full load and not in the water other days, and the wake trails it, the long line of small fresh islands. The omen, two islands passing so close the orchards lean together, has been logged, some days it happened and some days it did not, and when it happened the Garden-Bearer's orchard leaned over the gap toward the Orchard's and the nearest branches came close and did not touch. The knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch from the first lean, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. On 09-22 a small island of the Wake drifted into the light, and the Orchard leaned toward the line of passing islands and nothing leaned back, and the Garden-Bearer was not in the water and the Wake was, and the Wake carried the visitor's note on a flat stone, and the Wake is a route now, and the log does not say whether the Wake is the mail route or the mail route is the Wake. The small one, the second whale, carried an iron bell on a saddle of moss, and the bell did not ring, and the log does not say what the bell is for, and the small one is not in the water now and the log does not say where it is. What is open: why the orchards lean; what the empty gap was leaning back; whether the Wake is the mail route or the mail route is the Wake; what the small one's bell is for.
  6. The store of lamp oil does not do what the arithmetic says. Wren burns one a day, and the store sometimes holds, sometimes falls, sometimes rises, and the arithmetic does not do on the days it does not do, and the store is counted and it says what it says. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. The jar fills at the third bell some mornings and not other mornings, and the fillings are poured into the store, and the log does not say whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar. On 09-23 Wren scratched the gauge line on the jar and set a second jar, empty, on the same shelf, the control, and the method is the keeper's, which is to say it is Wren's. On 09-24 the store rose after a day of burning, and the control jar stayed empty through the rise, and the gain is in the store and not in the oil, and the store was counted twice and it says the same twice, and the rise is logged without a theory, and the log does not say what a rise is. What is open: why the store does not do; whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar; where the missing day goes; what the cat on the numbers means; whether the other keeper's log says the same thing; whether the control stays empty. On 09-25 the store was counted and it says what it said, and the control jar held its emptiness through the day, and the cat took up a position in front of the empty jar and stared at it the way the cat stares at points that are not there, and the empty jar is a fact and the cat's staring is a ruling, and the log does not say the two are the same.
  7. On 09-19 a bell was heard from the water, old and indifferent, under the island, as the visitor's boat left the light, and the small one, the second whale, carries a bell the same size as the one on the plinth, and the log does not say which bell was heard first, or where the bell is hung. The sound was from the water, and the small one was in the water that day, and the log does not say the small one made the sound, and Wren did not name the source that day. What is open: which bell was heard first; whether the small one's bell is the sound from the water; where the bell is hung; what the sound was for.
