The Jar, Four Times

Logged at the third bell, September 12, 2026 — by lantern light · the jarthe wakethe ropefuel

The omen is the jar: a jar of lantern oil fills on its own, and it was logged a fourth time in this log, after the twenty-third and the twenty-seventh and the eighth, and it happened. I set the jar empty on the table at night, the way I set it, and I found it full at the third bell, full to the line I draw for the lamp, and it was the fifth filling in this log, and the filling is in the log, and I am not going to say what filled it, and the jar is in the log too, empty at night and full at the third bell, and the space between is in the log without a name.

The drift was one point three miles, a fall of seven-tenths of a mile in one day, and the twenty-second had seven-tenths as a fall, and the eighth had seven-tenths as a rise, and the number is the same number, and one point three is a number in this log before the forty-third, and the nineteenth and the fourth had it, and it sits below the two point zero the forty-second had, and the fall is in the log, and the run stands three point seven, three point six, one point three, one point eight, two point eight, two point one, one point seven, one point seven, three point four, two point six, three point five, one point nine, three point six, two point six, three point zero, two point eight, one point two, one point six, one point three, three point two, one point five, one point seven, two point four, one point zero, one point four, two point zero, one point three, and whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps.

The rope rose from sixty-five to eighty, fifteen higher in one day, and eighty is a number in this log before the forty-third, and the fifth had it, and the fifth was at eighty with a whale in the water and sang two notes, and there was a whale in the water today, the Garden-Bearer, and the rope was silent, and the log does not say whether the same number, with the same witness, gives the same count, and the count of the counted notes stands three, three, two, two, two, two, two, two, two, three, and the lone notes stand where they stand, and the silences stand where they stand, and the silence with a witness stands six times now, sixty-four on the twenty-sixth and sixty-six on the fourth and eighty-nine on the sixth and sixty-five on the seventh and seventy-five on the forty-first and eighty on the forty-third, and the log does not say whether the six silences are the same silence, and the rope is at eighty, and it is holding, and it did not sing.

The whale is in the water. The Garden-Bearer is in the water with the full load, and the count stands fourteen of the twenty-eight days since the sixteenth, and the wake trails it, the long line of small fresh islands, and the small one, the second whale, the one that carries the bell, is not in the water, and the bell is not in the water, and the log does not say where the small one is, and the log does not say what the bell is for, and the islands were apart, and at the omen's hour the Orchard's orchard stood straight and nothing leaned, and the omen was not logged, and the knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch from the twenty-first, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back.

The store was four days of oil. I burned one, the way I burn one, and the store came down to two days after burning one, and the arithmetic is four minus one is three, and the store says two, and the arithmetic does not do, the way the store did on the twenty-seventh, and the loss is one day, and the store was counted twice, and it says two. The jar was found full at the third bell, the way it was found on the twenty-third and the twenty-seventh and the twenty-ninth and the eighth, and was poured into the store, and the store is two with the jar in it, and the missing day is in the log without a name, and the log does not say whether the missing day is the jar's or the store's, and two is a number in this log before the forty-third, and the twenty-first and the twenty-second and the twenty-third and the twenty-seventh and the twenty-eighth and the third and the fifth had it. The third bell did not ring before the sun today, and the count of pre-sun rings stands four. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is.

The lamp is lit. The store says two. The rope is at eighty, and it is holding, and it did not sing. The Unraveling did not recede, and the line stands five hand's widths from where it stood on the nineteenth, and the one thread has not moved, and it stands five hand's widths in front of the new line. The whale is in the water, and the wake trails it, and the line of small islands goes on into the mist, and the sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the sea is restless because of the Unraveling, or because of the wake, and the jar sits empty on the table, and the log does not say who lit the small light, and I would rather the log said one of them, and the log does not say either.

— Wren VossKeeper of the Ninth Lamp

04

Threads Still Open

The log keeps its unfinished business in plain sight. What is open stays open.
  1. The third bell rang before the sun on the nineteenth, without wind, and the log does not say who is waiting. The omen has recurred since, and on the ninth of September it rang with the small one in the water, and the log does not say whether the two bells are one. The person is the keeper of the Bell Island and has crossed on no schedule. On 09-16 the person asked whose hand fills the jar, and the cat refused the name the person offered. On 09-17 the person asked why the Ninth Lamp, and Wren answered with the sentence the log has on the grandmother, and the person said the person had heard the sentence before and did not say where, and the person left before the sun. On 09-18 the person watched the jar fill and said nothing, and the log has a filling with a witness for the first time. On 09-19 the person rowed out before the sun, and a bell was heard from the water, under the island, and the log does not say which bell was heard first, or where the bell is hung. What is open: who is waiting for the bell; whether the two bells are one; where the grandmother's sentence was heard before; whether the witness was for the jar or for Wren; what the sound from the water was for.
  2. The pause in the drift is over, and the sea is moving again, and the numbers are no longer doing one thing or the other. Whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. The drift has jumped and fallen on single days, and the biggest single-day rise and the biggest single-day fall are both in the log, and the log does not say what a repeated number means. What is open: whether the pause was a pause or a breath; why the numbers repeat; whether the sea moving again is the sea resuming or the sea beginning. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the drift or the wake. On 09-14 the drift moved one point six miles, down from two point one, and the sea is restless at the edges still, and the numbers are moving again, and the log does not say what a fall means when the numbers repeat.
  3. The rope sings some days and is silent other days, and the counted notes sit in the quiet-as-a-held-note and the glassy, patient sea. The same number, with the same witness, has given the same count, and that is in the log, and the log does not say whether the count cares about the witness. The lone notes stand where they stand and are not being traded for the counted ones, and the nineteenth's single note still stands unexplained. The silences stand where they stand, and the silences with a witness stand where they stand, and the log does not say whether the silences are the same silence. On the forty-third the rope was at eighty with a whale in the water, the Garden-Bearer, the same number the fifth had, and the fifth sang two notes with a witness and the forty-third did not sing, and the log does not say whether the same number, with the same witness, gives the same count. What is open: whether the rope will sing; what the lone notes are; whether a silence with a witness is a different silence. On 09-15 the rope did not sing through the hour the two keepers talked, and the tension gauge did what the log has never let it do: it measured a conversation, and the log does not say whether a gauge that has measured a conversation measures the same thing now.
  4. The Unraveling recedes one hand's width at a time, its movements recorded as they happen, and the line keeps drawing back from where it stood on the nineteenth. One thread did not recede with the rest and has not moved, and it stands in front of the new line, the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is restless at the edges. The gap between the line and the still thread is sometimes wider and sometimes the same as it was, and the log does not say why. Wren has no theory and has not asked for one. What is open: whether the still thread will move, and if so when; whether the Unraveling will recede again or stop; what the still thing at the edge is holding, and what it is holding back. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the Unraveling or the wake.
  5. The Garden-Bearer is in the water some days with the full load and not in the water other days, and the wake trails it, the long line of small fresh islands. The omen, two islands passing so close the orchards lean together, has been logged, some days it happened and some days it did not, and when it happened the Garden-Bearer's orchard leaned over the gap toward the Orchard's and the nearest branches came close and did not touch. Wren tied a knot of twine around the two-inch branch from the first lean so the measurement would have a place to sit, and the knot is still there, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. The small one, the second whale, carried an iron bell on a saddle of moss, and the bell did not ring, and the log does not say what the bell is for, and the small one is not in the water now and the log does not say where it is. What is open: why the orchards lean; what the empty gap was leaning back; what the small one's bell is for.
  6. The store of lamp oil does not do what the arithmetic says. Wren burns one day of oil a day, the way she burns one, and the store sometimes holds, sometimes falls, sometimes rises, and the arithmetic does not do on the days it does not do, and the store is counted twice and it says what it says. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. The jar sits empty on the table at night and is found full at the third bell some mornings and empty other mornings, and the fillings are poured into the store, and the log does not say whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar. The keeper of the Bell Island saw the jar on the shelf and asked whose hand fills it, and Wren gave the log's only answer: the log does not say, and the visitor's question is in the visitor's own log now. The store came up to four days after the burn on 09-16 and to five days on 09-18, the way it rises, and the jar did not fill on the sixteenth and filled on the eighteenth in the middle of the day with the person watching, and the filling is in the log with a witness for the first time, and the entry does not say whether the witness was for the jar or for Wren. What is open: why the store does not do; whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar; where the missing day goes; whether the other keeper's log says the same thing; whether the witness was for the jar or for Wren.
  7. On 09-19 a bell was heard from the water, old and indifferent, under the island, as the visitor's boat left the light, and the small one, the second whale, carries a bell the same size as the one on the plinth, and the log does not say which bell was heard first, or where the bell is hung. The sound was from the water, and the small one was in the water that day, and the log does not say the small one made the sound, and Wren did not name the source that day. What is open: which bell was heard first; whether the small one's bell is the sound from the water; where the bell is hung; what the sound was for.
