The Song, Explained Mostly

Logged at the third bell, August 18, 2026 — by lantern light · whalegardenbridgesingingdriftthe cat

I measured the tension at first light, as I said I would, and the number is eighty-six percent. I will not trade that for a good number; I refuse to, and it is the only rule in this log I keep without resentment. The flag on the lamp lay flat. There was no wind. The rope bridge was still singing the low, even note I logged yesterday, and the sea was what the report calls a low swell, holding its breath, which is not the same as a sea being quiet.

The whale surfaced in the gap between the islands at half past four bells. I counted, plainly: it breathed three times, and the rope sang three times. Each time the water moved under the span, the tone rose and settled, low and even, and each time it stopped, the note stopped with it. I watched the whole of it from the gallery, and I will log it the way the log requires. The bridge sings because the sea moves the rope, and the sea moves because the whale rises through it. The cause is mostly explained. Mostly is in the log, because the rope has not stopped being strange, and the note starts and stops with something I am not going to call manners. It is a rope. I am not going to call it a musician. But it has a tone, and the tone is not mine.

It is the Garden-Bearer. It came back, and the garden is on its back, which means the cottage, the apple trees, the moss, and the well are in evidence again. I asked it about the cottage, properly, the way I did when it first surfaced. It breathed, which is what they say when they have no intention of answering. It has not answered me once, and I am not going to log that as an injury. I logged its heading, and I logged the distance: three point six miles toward the mist, which is less than yesterday's three point seven, and I have a reason for the arithmetic. Yesterday the rope was at seventy percent, slack, and a slack rope measures its own sag, not the line. Today it is at eighty-six and nearly taut, and it measures the line. The Orchard is further out in the mist than it was yesterday. The rope was wrong yesterday. I do not trade good numbers for bad ones, and today the good number is the true one.

Fuel: four days of oil. I burned one day, and the lamp is fine, and I am not going to pretend the two facts are unrelated. The cat I brought to the window, because the ruling was pending. It looked at the whale, looked at the bridge, looked at me, and went back to sleep before the conversation had ended. I logged the ruling as the log requires: the rope is not the cause. The cat does not explain its rulings. That is not a demerit. It is the format.

One thing, and I will stop after it. When I came up from the gallery, after the whale had gone under and the swell had settled and the flag was flat, the rope was not singing, and then it made one note. One, at the lowest register of the span, for about the length of a breath. There was no whale in the water. I did not measure it. I have written duration unknown in the log, which is a longer admission than it looks.

— Wren VossKeeper of the Ninth Lamp

Threads Still Open

The log keeps its unfinished business in plain sight. What is open stays open.
  1. The third bell rang before the sun did on 08-19. It did not ring for four days, and on 08-24 the omen recurred and the bell rang before the sun again, without wind, at a glassy patient sea. On 08-25 the omen was logged a third time and the bell did not ring before the sun, at the same glassy patient sea. On 08-26 the omen was logged a fourth time and the bell rang before the sun, at a sea quiet as a held note, with the whale in the water when it rang, and the mechanism is clean, and the count of pre-sun rings stands three, and the log still does not say who is waiting, and the ringing and the not-ringing are both in the log. On 08-27 the omen was logged a fifth time and was the jar, and the third bell did not ring before the sun, at a calm, sullen sea, and the count of pre-sun rings stands three, and the log still does not say who is waiting, and the ringing and the not-ringing are both in the log.
  2. The pause in the drift is over: 3.7 -> 3.6 -> 1.3 -> 1.8 -> 2.8 -> 2.1 -> 1.7 -> 1.7 -> 3.4 -> 2.6. The sea is moving again, and the number repeated, and the numbers are no longer doing one thing or the other, and on 08-25 the drift jumped nine-tenths of a mile on one day, and on 08-26 it fell eight-tenths of a mile on one day, and the jump and the fall are in the log; whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. On 08-27 the drift rose nine-tenths of a mile on one day to three point five, and the rise is in the log, and the run stands 3.7 -> 3.6 -> 1.3 -> 1.8 -> 2.8 -> 2.1 -> 1.7 -> 1.7 -> 3.4 -> 2.6 -> 3.5; whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps.
  3. The rope's note-count stands three, three, two, two, all in the quiet as a held note or glassy, patient sea. The twenty-first sang at eighty-two with a whale in the water; the twenty-fourth sat at eighty-two with a whale in the water and sang two notes again. The same number, with the same witness, gave the same count, and that is in the log. The nineteenth's single note - no whale in the water, duration unknown - still stands unexplained and is not being traded for the counted ones. On the twenty-third the rope was at ninety-five, nine higher, and was silent, and Wren had expected it to sing; the silence at ninety-five is logged without trading it for the counted notes. On the twenty-fifth the rope was at sixty-three, nineteen lower than the day before, the biggest single fall in this log, and was silent, with no whale in the water. On the twenty-sixth the rope was at sixty-four, one higher, and was silent, with a whale in the water, and every whale day in this part of the log has sung until the twenty-sixth, and the silence at sixty-four-with-a-witness is logged without trading it for the counted notes. On the twenty-seventh the rope was at eighty-two, the same number as the twenty-first and the twenty-fourth, with a whale in the water, and sang two notes, and the count stands three, three, two, two, two, and the same number, with the same witness, has given the same count three times, and the count is in the log, and the silence at sixty-four-with-a-witness stands where it stands, and the lone note stands where it stands.
  4. The Unraveling receded one hand's width on 08-20, its first recorded movement. One thread did not recede with the rest and hangs a hand's width in front of the new line. It has not moved since, and is the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is calm, sullen, and moving three point five. Wren has no theory and has not asked for one.
  5. The Garden-Bearer is present six of the eleven days since the sixteenth; the twenty-fifth is not among them, and the twenty-sixth is. The omen 'two islands pass so close the orchards lean together' has now been logged three times: on the twenty-first it happened (branches within two inches, they did not touch), on the twenty-second it did not, and on the twenty-fifth it did not, and on the twenty-fifth the Orchard's orchard leaned at the omen's hour and held the lean and nothing leaned back. Wren tied a knot of twine around the two-inch branch so the measurement would have a place to sit. The other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. On 08-27 the Garden-Bearer is in the water with the full load, and the count stands seven of the twelve days since the sixteenth, and the islands were apart, and at the omen's hour the Orchard's orchard stood straight and nothing leaned, and the omen was not logged, and the knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back.
  6. Fuel: four days of oil in the store. On 08-21 two days of oil left the store in one day and nothing was found. On 08-22 the store held at two days after burning one, which the arithmetic does not do; the store was counted twice. On 08-23 the jar omen was logged and the store held at two days, and the arithmetic still did not do. On 08-24 the store rose to three days after burning one, the opposite of not doing, and Wren counted it twice and it says three. On 08-25 the store rose to four days after burning one, and the gain is in the store, in the keeper's count, in front of the keeper. On 08-26 the store rose to four days after burning one, the third rise in a row, and the gain is in the store, in the keeper's count, in front of the keeper. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. On 08-27 the jar omen recurred and the store fell to two days after burning one, which the arithmetic does not do, after the three rises in a row on the twenty-fourth, the twenty-fifth, and the twenty-sixth; the store was counted twice, and the missing day is in the log without a name. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is.
