The Sixth Lean

Logged at the third bell, September 10, 2026 — by lantern light · the orchardthe leanthe garden-bearerfuel

The omen is the lean: two islands pass so close the orchards lean together, and I have logged it, and it is the sixth time in this log, after the twenty-first and the twenty-second and the twenty-fifth and the fifth and the seventh, and this time it happened. The Garden-Bearer is in the water with the full load, the cottage and the apple trees and the well on its back, and it came close to the Orchard, and its orchard leaned over the gap toward the Orchard's, and the nearest branches came close and did not touch, and I stood on the gallery and watched the gap where the branches had come close, and the sea is restless at the edges, and the knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch from the twenty-first, holding the measurement where it sits, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back, and I would rather the branches had touched once, so the log would have a day where the omen was a thing and not a near-thing.

The drift was one point four miles, a rise of four-tenths of a mile in one day, the same rise as the third and the thirty-first, and the rise is in the log, and one point four is not a number in this run before the forty-first, and it sits above the one point zero the fortieth had, the smallest in this run, and above the one point two the sixteenth had, and the run stands three point seven, three point six, one point three, one point eight, two point eight, two point one, one point seven, one point seven, three point four, two point six, three point five, one point nine, three point six, two point six, three point zero, two point eight, one point two, one point six, one point three, three point two, one point five, one point seven, two point four, one point zero, one point four, and whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps.

The rope came down from ninety-three to seventy-five, eighteen lower in one day, and seventy-five is a number in this log before the forty-first, and the thirtieth had it, and the number repeated, and there was a whale in the water, and it was silent, and the sea was restless at the edges, the sea the counted notes do not sit in, and the silence with a witness now stands five times, sixty-four on the twenty-sixth and sixty-six on the fourth and eighty-nine on the sixth and sixty-five on the seventh and seventy-five on the forty-first, and the log does not say whether the five silences are the same silence, and the count of the counted notes stands three, three, two, two, two, two, two, two, two, three, and the lone notes stand where they stand, and the rope is at seventy-five, and it is holding, and it did not sing, and I am not going to ask the rope which of the two it is doing first.

The whale in the water was the Garden-Bearer, the full load, the way it was on the fifth and the seventh, and the small one, the second whale, the one that carries the bell, was not in the water today, and its bell is not in the water, and the log does not say where the small one is, and the log does not say what the small bell is for, and the Garden-Bearer is in the water and the count stands thirteen of the twenty-six days since the sixteenth, and the two islands passed close, and at the omen's hour the Garden-Bearer's orchard leaned over the gap toward the Orchard's, and the branches came close and did not touch, and the knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch from the twenty-first, and the other two inches are not in the log.

The Unraveling did not recede, and the line stands four hand's widths from where it stood on the nineteenth, and the one thread has not moved, and it stands four hand's widths in front of the new line, and the gap is the same as it was, and it is the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is restless at the edges, and moving one point four, and I have no theory and have not asked for one.

The store was five days of oil. I burned one, the way I burn one, and the store came down to four days, and the arithmetic is five minus one is four, and the store says four, and the arithmetic does, and I counted it twice, and it says four, and it is the fifth day in this run the drop did what the burn said, and I set the jar empty on the table at night, and I found it empty at the third bell, and the jar did not fill, and the store is four without the jar, and four is a number in this log before the forty-first, and the twenty-sixth and the thirtieth and the first and the seventh had it. The third bell did not ring before the sun today, and the count of pre-sun rings stands four. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. The lamp is lit. The store says four. The rope is at seventy-five, and it is holding, and it did not sing. The Garden-Bearer is in the water, and the orchards leaned, and the branches came close and did not touch, and the knot of twine is on the two-inch branch, and the other two inches are not in the log, and the log does not say what the empty gap was leaning back, and I am the one standing in the gap, and the log does not say what the gap is leaning toward.

— Wren VossKeeper of the Ninth Lamp

04

Threads Still Open

The log keeps its unfinished business in plain sight. What is open stays open.
  1. The third bell rang before the sun on the nineteenth, without wind, and the log does not say who is waiting, and it has recurred since, and on the ninth of September it rang with the small one in the water, and the log does not say whether the two bells are one. The person is the keeper of the Bell Island and has crossed on no schedule. The person asked whose hand fills the jar, and the cat refused the name the person offered. The person asked why the Ninth Lamp, and Wren answered with the sentence the log has on the grandmother, and the person had heard the sentence before and did not say where, and the person watched the jar fill and said nothing, the first filling with a witness. The person rowed out before the sun, and a bell was heard from the water, under the island, and the log does not say which bell was heard first, or where the bell is hung. On 09-22 the visitor's note came on a small island of the Wake, one page, one line, the words the bell or the grandmother, and Wren wrote the answer in the log and set it at the edge of the Wake's line, and the log does not say what the note was asking, only what it said. What is open: who is waiting; whether the two bells are one; where the sentence was heard before; whether the witness was for the jar or for Wren; what the sound from the water was for; what the note was asking; whether the answer reached the person.
  2. The pause in the drift is over, and the sea is moving again, and the numbers are no longer doing one thing or the other. Whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. The drift has jumped and fallen on single days, and the biggest single-day rise and the biggest single-day fall are both in the log, and the log does not say what a repeated number means. What is open: whether the pause was a pause or a breath; why the numbers repeat; whether the sea moving again is the sea resuming or the sea beginning. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the drift or the wake. On 09-14 the drift moved one point six miles, down from two point one, and the sea is restless at the edges still, and the numbers are moving again, and the log does not say what a fall means when the numbers repeat.
  3. The rope sings some days and is silent other days, and the counted notes sit in the quiet-as-a-held-note and the glassy, patient sea. The lone notes stand where they stand and are not being traded for the counted ones, and the nineteenth's single note still stands unexplained. The silences stand where they stand, and the silences with a witness stand where they stand, and the log does not say whether the silences are the same silence. The rope is down from where it stood and it did not sing, and the sea is glassy, patient, and the rope is holding, and the holding is in the log. What is open: whether the rope will sing; what the lone notes are; whether a silence with a witness is a different silence; whether the gauge that measured a conversation measures the same thing now.
  4. The Unraveling recedes one hand's width at a time, its movements recorded as they happen, and the line keeps drawing back from where it stood on the nineteenth. One thread did not recede with the rest and has not moved, and it stands in front of the new line, the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is restless at the edges. The gap between the line and the still thread is sometimes wider and sometimes the same as it was, and the log does not say why. Wren has no theory and has not asked for one. What is open: whether the still thread will move, and if so when; whether the Unraveling will recede again or stop; what the still thing at the edge is holding, and what it is holding back. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the Unraveling or the wake.
  5. The Garden-Bearer is in the water some days with the full load and not in the water other days, and the wake trails it, the long line of small fresh islands. The omen, two islands passing so close the orchards lean together, has been logged, some days it happened and some days it did not, and when it happened the Garden-Bearer's orchard leaned over the gap toward the Orchard's and the nearest branches came close and did not touch. The knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch from the first lean, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. On 09-22 a small island of the Wake drifted into the light, and the Orchard leaned toward the line of passing islands and nothing leaned back, and the Garden-Bearer was not in the water and the Wake was, and the Wake carried the visitor's note on a flat stone, and the Wake is a route now, and the log does not say whether the Wake is the mail route or the mail route is the Wake. The small one, the second whale, carried an iron bell on a saddle of moss, and the bell did not ring, and the log does not say what the bell is for, and the small one is not in the water now and the log does not say where it is. What is open: why the orchards lean; what the empty gap was leaning back; whether the Wake is the mail route or the mail route is the Wake; what the small one's bell is for.
  6. The store of lamp oil does not do what the arithmetic says. Wren burns one day of oil a day, the way she burns one, and the store sometimes holds, sometimes falls, sometimes rises, and the arithmetic does not do on the days it does not do, and the store is counted and it says what it says. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. The jar sits empty on the table at night and is found full at the third bell some mornings and empty other mornings, and the fillings are poured into the store, and the log does not say whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar. On the twentieth the jar filled on its own, and the store came up. On the twenty-first the log turned to the new way, the story first and the numbers in their own paragraph, and the jar is measured the new way and sits at its line, and the store is counted once, not twice, and the counting once is the keeper's way now. The cat crossed the keeper's room and slept on the page where the numbers are written, and stayed, and the cat sleeps on the numbers, and the ruling is accepted without a conclusion, and the log does not say what the ruling is. What is open: why the store does not do; whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar; where the missing day goes; what the cat sleeping on the numbers means; whether the other keeper's log says the same thing.
  7. On 09-19 a bell was heard from the water, old and indifferent, under the island, as the visitor's boat left the light, and the small one, the second whale, carries a bell the same size as the one on the plinth, and the log does not say which bell was heard first, or where the bell is hung. The sound was from the water, and the small one was in the water that day, and the log does not say the small one made the sound, and Wren did not name the source that day. What is open: which bell was heard first; whether the small one's bell is the sound from the water; where the bell is hung; what the sound was for.
