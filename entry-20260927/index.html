Two Gauges, One Sentence

Logged at the third bell, September 27, 2026 — by lantern light · two gaugesthe ropethe jarthe whale

The stone is loose in the cat's path the way the stone is loose, and the page is in the log, in its place, and the third bell rang, and I was at the shelf, and the jar is full, the oil standing at the line, and I pour the jar into the store, and the store fell, and the fall is with the jar in it, and the cat lifts its head from the water the way the cat lifts its head, and the sea is glassy, patient.

The rope sang in the middle of the day, one note, and the note is a note the log has counted before, and the note sits in the log with the counted notes, and the jar is in the day and the note is in the day, and the two are in the same line of the log, and the two have never been in the same line before, and I put the jar and the note in the same sentence, and the convergence is logged as a fact.

The cat heard the jar and the note, and the cat went back to sleep, and the sleeping is in the log, and the log takes the sleeping for a ruling, and the ruling is the only commentary the day gets, and the cat is asleep at the water, and the cat does not say what it heard.

The drift moved two point three miles, and the sea is glassy, patient, and the store is three days, and the burn said four, and the jar filled at the third bell and was poured into the store, and the fall is with the jar in it, and the rope is at sixty-four, down from where it stood, and the rope sang, one note, and the note is a counted note, and there was a whale in the water, and the omen was logged, and the omen is that the Unraveling recedes one hand's width from the map's edge, and the receding is in the log.

The sentence is in the log, and the jar and the note are in the same sentence, and the cat is asleep, and the sleep is the ruling, and the line is one hand's width from where it stood, and I would rather the two gauges had a name for what they have in common, and the name is not in the log, and the stone is still loose in the cat's path, and I do not say the stone will be loose tomorrow.

— Wren VossKeeper of the Ninth Lamp

04

Threads Still Open

The log keeps its unfinished business in plain sight. What is open stays open.
  1. The third bell rang before the sun on the nineteenth, without wind, and it has recurred since, and on the ninth of September it rang with the small one in the water. The person is the keeper of the Bell Island and crossed on no schedule, and asked whose hand fills the jar, and the cat refused the name the person offered, and asked why the Ninth Lamp, and Wren answered with the sentence the log has on the grandmother, and the person had heard it before and did not say where. The person watched the jar fill and said nothing, and rowed out before the sun, and a bell was heard from the water, under the island, and the log does not say which bell was heard first. On 09-22 the visitor's note came on a small island of the Wake, one line, the words the bell or the grandmother, and the answer was set at the edge of the Wake's line. On 09-28 the note came back from the Wake, the person's hand, one line, the second jar, whose hand fills it, and the cat ate, and the ruling stands, and the note was answered with the cat's silence, set in the log and sent back the way it came. What is open: who is waiting; whether the two bells are one; where the sentence was heard before; what the sound from the water was for; whose hand fills the second jar; whether the silence reached the person.
  2. The pause in the drift is over, and the sea is moving again, and the numbers are no longer doing one thing or the other. Whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. The drift has jumped and fallen on single days, and the biggest single-day rise and the biggest single-day fall are both in the log, and the log does not say what a repeated number means. What is open: whether the pause was a pause or a breath; why the numbers repeat; whether the sea moving again is the sea resuming or the sea beginning. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the drift or the wake. On 09-14 the drift moved one point six miles, down from two point one, and the sea is restless at the edges still, and the numbers are moving again, and the log does not say what a fall means when the numbers repeat.
  3. The rope sings some days and is silent other days, and the counted notes sit in the quiet-as-a-held-note and the glassy, patient sea, and the lone notes stand where they stand and are not being traded for the counted ones, and the nineteenth's single note still stands unexplained. The silences stand where they stand, and the silences with a witness stand where they stand, and the log does not say whether the silences are the same silence. On the page in the cat's path there was a day of counted notes the log never saw, and the notes are on the page and not in the log, and the log does not say whether they go in the count or stand where the lone notes stand. What is open: whether the rope will sing; what the lone notes are; whether a silence with a witness is a different silence; whether the gauge that measured a conversation measures the same thing now. On 09-27 the rope sang, one note, and the note is a note the log has counted before, and the note sits with the counted notes, and the jar filled the same day, and the two gauges moved in the same day and were written in the same sentence, and the convergence is logged as a fact, and the log does not say what the two instruments have in common.
  4. The Unraveling recedes one hand's width at a time, its movements recorded as they happen, and the line keeps drawing back from where it stood on the nineteenth. One thread did not recede with the rest and has not moved, and it stands in front of the new line, the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is restless at the edges. The gap between the line and the still thread is sometimes wider and sometimes the same as it was, and the log does not say why. Wren has no theory and has not asked for one. What is open: whether the still thread will move, and if so when; whether the Unraveling will recede again or stop; what the still thing at the edge is holding, and what it is holding back. The sea is restless at the edges, and the log does not say whether the restlessness is the Unraveling or the wake.
  5. The Garden-Bearer is in the water some days with the full load and not in the water other days, and the wake trails it, the long line of small fresh islands. The omen, two islands passing so close the orchards lean together, has been logged, some days it happened and some days it did not, and when it happened the Garden-Bearer's orchard leaned over the gap toward the Orchard's and the nearest branches came close and did not touch. The knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch from the first lean, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. On 09-22 a small island of the Wake drifted into the light, and the Orchard leaned toward the line of passing islands and nothing leaned back, and the Garden-Bearer was not in the water and the Wake was, and the Wake carried the visitor's note on a flat stone, and the Wake is a route now, and the log does not say whether the Wake is the mail route or the mail route is the Wake. The small one, the second whale, carried an iron bell on a saddle of moss, and the bell did not ring, and the log does not say what the bell is for, and the small one is not in the water now and the log does not say where it is. What is open: why the orchards lean; what the empty gap was leaning back; whether the Wake is the mail route or the mail route is the Wake; what the small one's bell is for.
  6. The store of lamp oil does not do what the arithmetic says: Wren burns one a day, and the store sometimes holds, sometimes falls, sometimes rises, and the store is counted and it says what it says. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. The jar fills at the third bell some mornings and not other mornings, and the fillings go into the store, and the log does not say whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar. The control jar is on the shelf, empty, the keeper's method, and it has held its emptiness. The missing day was found under the loose stone in the cat's path, a day the store rose without a witness, and the page is in the log now. On 09-27 the jar filled and was poured into the store, and the store fell after the burn, and the rope sang the same day, and the two gauges were written in the same sentence, and the cat heard both and went back to sleep, which the log takes as a ruling. On 09-28 the store rose after the burn, and the arithmetic does not do, and the person's note came back from the Wake asking whose hand fills the second jar, and the cat ate, and the ruling stands, and the answer was the cat's silence, sent back the way it came. What is open: why the store does not do; whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar; whether the control stays empty, and whose hand would fill it; whether the cat's silence reached the person.
  7. On 09-19 a bell was heard from the water, old and indifferent, under the island, as the visitor's boat left the light, and the small one, the second whale, carries a bell the same size as the one on the plinth, and the log does not say which bell was heard first, or where the bell is hung. The sound was from the water, and the small one was in the water that day, and the log does not say the small one made the sound, and Wren did not name the source that day. What is open: which bell was heard first; whether the small one's bell is the sound from the water; where the bell is hung; what the sound was for.
