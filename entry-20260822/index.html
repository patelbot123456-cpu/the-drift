The Omen, Unanswered

Logged at the third bell, August 22, 2026 — by lantern light · omenorchardgarden-bearersinging bridgefuelthe catthe unravelingdrift

The Garden-Bearer is not in the water. I checked at first light, and the water is empty, and the log's column for days present holds four of the seven since the sixteenth, and the seventh day is today, and it is a no. The omen for the day is in the log: two islands pass so close the orchards lean together. The omen is the same as yesterday's, and yesterday the omen happened, and the nearest branches came to within two inches, and they did not touch, and today the carrier of one of the orchards is not in the water. I am logging the omen as written, and I am logging the empty water as seen, and the log has a column for omens, and the log does not have a column for an omen that did not happen, so the two facts are sitting in the same entry, and they will sit there.

The drift was two point one miles. The log has the run: three point seven, three point six, one point three, one point eight, two point eight, two point one, and the pause is over, and a falling number does not bring a pause back. I have the number. The rope came off eighty-two and sat at eighty-six, and it was silent. On the eighteenth it was at eighty-six with a whale in the water, and it sang, and today it is at eighty-six with no whale in the water, and it did not sing, and the sea is what the report calls a low swell, holding its breath, which is the eighteenth's sea, and the sea is the same and the water is not. The count stands three, three, two, and the lone note stands where it stands: one note, no whale in the water, duration unknown. I am not trading any of it for any of the rest.

At the hour the omen says, the Orchard's orchard leaned. I was on the gallery, because the omen was in the log, and the log says to watch what the log says will happen. The branches leaned over the gap, toward the water, low and slow, the way a branch leans when there is something to lean toward, and I caught myself, because there was nothing to lean toward, and the water was empty. The branch that had come to within two inches on the twenty-first — the one I tied a knot of twine around, because a measurement needs a place to sit — leaned first, and it held the lean. Nothing leaned back. The omen has two halves, and one of them happened, and I am logging the lean without the other half of it, because the log does not have a column for what the orchard was leaning toward.

Fuel: two days. I burned one day, and the arithmetic says one, and the gauge says two, and I counted the store twice, the way I counted it yesterday, and it says two. The store held. Yesterday two days of oil left the store in one day and nothing was found, and today the store does not decrease at all, and I am logging the holding without a theory of the holding. The gauge has been right in this log, and I am keeping its number, because I refuse to trade a good number for a bad one, and two days is a good number for a keeper with two days. The cat I brought to the window at the third bell, because the ruling on the holding store was pending. It looked at the store, looked at me, and went back to sleep, which I am logging as a ruling that the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. The bell has not rung since the nineteenth, and I have not rung it either. The thread at the map's edge is still hanging, and it has not moved, and it is the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is moving.

In the evening the orchard was straight again. I went out to the gallery and looked at the gap, and the gap was water, and the water was doing what the report says it is doing. The knot of twine is on the branch, and I am not taking it down, because a measurement that has not been matched is still a measurement, and the log holds the two inches, and the log will hold them for as long as the other two inches are not there. I would rather the log ended on the lamp. The lamp is lit. It ends on the knot.

— Wren VossKeeper of the Ninth Lamp

Threads Still Open

The log keeps its unfinished business in plain sight. What is open stays open.
  1. The third bell rang before the sun did on 08-19. It did not ring for four days, and on 08-24 the omen recurred and the bell rang before the sun again, without wind, at a glassy patient sea. On 08-25 the omen was logged a third time and the bell did not ring before the sun, at the same glassy patient sea. On 08-26 the omen was logged a fourth time and the bell rang before the sun, at a sea quiet as a held note, with the whale in the water when it rang, and the mechanism is clean, and the count of pre-sun rings stands three, and the log still does not say who is waiting, and the ringing and the not-ringing are both in the log. On 08-27 the omen was logged a fifth time and was the jar, and the third bell did not ring before the sun, at a calm, sullen sea, and the count of pre-sun rings stands three, and the log still does not say who is waiting, and the ringing and the not-ringing are both in the log. On 08-28 the omen was the receding and the third bell did not ring before the sun, at a low swell, holding its breath, and the count of pre-sun rings stands three, and the log still does not say who is waiting, and the ringing and the not-ringing are both in the log. On 08-29 the omen was the cat staring at a point on the horizon that is not there, and the third bell did not ring before the sun, at a sea quiet as a held note, and the count of pre-sun rings stands three, and the log still does not say who is waiting, and the ringing and the not-ringing are both in the log, and the point is not in the chart, and the log does not say what the point is.
  2. The pause in the drift is over: 3.7 -> 3.6 -> 1.3 -> 1.8 -> 2.8 -> 2.1 -> 1.7 -> 1.7 -> 3.4 -> 2.6. The sea is moving again, and the number repeated, and the numbers are no longer doing one thing or the other, and on 08-25 the drift jumped nine-tenths of a mile on one day, and on 08-26 it fell eight-tenths of a mile on one day, and the jump and the fall are in the log; whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. On 08-27 the drift rose nine-tenths of a mile on one day to three point five, and the rise is in the log, and the run stands 3.7 -> 3.6 -> 1.3 -> 1.8 -> 2.8 -> 2.1 -> 1.7 -> 1.7 -> 3.4 -> 2.6 -> 3.5; whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. On 08-28 the drift fell six-tenths of a mile on one day to one point nine, and the fall is in the log, and the run stands 3.7 -> 3.6 -> 1.3 -> 1.8 -> 2.8 -> 2.1 -> 1.7 -> 1.7 -> 3.4 -> 2.6 -> 3.5 -> 1.9; whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps. On 08-29 the drift rose one point seven miles on one day to three point six, the biggest single-day rise in this log, tied with the twenty-fifth, and the number is the same number the eighteenth had, and the rise is in the log, and the run stands 3.7 -> 3.6 -> 1.3 -> 1.8 -> 2.8 -> 2.1 -> 1.7 -> 1.7 -> 3.4 -> 2.6 -> 3.5 -> 1.9 -> 3.6; whether the pause was a pause or a breath is untested, and the number is the only evidence the log keeps.
  3. The rope's note-count stands three, three, two, two, all in the quiet as a held note or glassy, patient sea. The twenty-first sang at eighty-two with a whale in the water; the twenty-fourth sat at eighty-two with a whale in the water and sang two notes again. The same number, with the same witness, gave the same count, and that is in the log. The nineteenth's single note - no whale in the water, duration unknown - still stands unexplained and is not being traded for the counted ones. On the twenty-third the rope was at ninety-five, nine higher, and was silent, and Wren had expected it to sing; the silence at ninety-five is logged without trading it for the counted notes. On the twenty-fifth the rope was at sixty-three, nineteen lower than the day before, the biggest single fall in this log, and was silent, with no whale in the water. On the twenty-sixth the rope was at sixty-four, one higher, and was silent, with a whale in the water, and every whale day in this part of the log has sung until the twenty-sixth, and the silence at sixty-four-with-a-witness is logged without trading it for the counted notes. On the twenty-seventh the rope was at eighty-two, the same number as the twenty-first and the twenty-fourth, with a whale in the water, and sang two notes, and the count stands three, three, two, two, two, and the same number, with the same witness, has given the same count three times, and the count is in the log, and the silence at sixty-four-with-a-witness stands where it stands, and the lone note stands where it stands. On the twenty-eighth the rope was at ninety-five, thirteen higher than the twenty-seventh, the same number as the twenty-third, and was silent, with no whale in the water, and the silence at ninety-five is in the log twice now, and both times without a witness, and the count stands three, three, two, two, two, and the lone note stands where it stands, and the silence at sixty-four-with-a-witness stands where it stands. On the twenty-ninth the rope was at eighty-seven, eight lower than the twenty-eighth, and eighty-seven is not a number in this log before the twenty-ninth, and there was a whale in the water, and it sang two notes, in the quiet-as-a-held-note sea the counted notes sit in, and the count stands three, three, two, two, two, two, and the lone note stands where it stands, and the silence at sixty-four-with-a-witness stands where it stands.
  4. The Unraveling receded one hand's width on 08-20, its first recorded movement. One thread did not recede with the rest and hangs a hand's width in front of the new line. On 08-28 the Unraveling receded one hand's width again, its second recorded movement, and the line is two hand's widths now from where it stood on the nineteenth, and the one thread has not moved, and it now stands two hand's widths in front of the new line, and the gap is one hand's width wider than it was, and it is the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is a low swell, holding its breath, and moving one point nine. Wren has no theory and has not asked for one. On 08-29 the Unraveling did not recede, and the line stands two hand's widths from where it stood on the nineteenth, and the one thread has not moved, and it stands two hand's widths in front of the new line, and it is the only still thing at the edge of a sea that is quiet as a held note, and moving three point six, and Wren has no theory and has not asked for one.
  5. The Garden-Bearer is present six of the eleven days since the sixteenth; the twenty-fifth is not among them, and the twenty-sixth is. The omen 'two islands pass so close the orchards lean together' has now been logged three times: on the twenty-first it happened (branches within two inches, they did not touch), on the twenty-second it did not, and on the twenty-fifth it did not, and on the twenty-fifth the Orchard's orchard leaned at the omen's hour and held the lean and nothing leaned back. Wren tied a knot of twine around the two-inch branch so the measurement would have a place to sit. The other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. On 08-27 the Garden-Bearer is in the water with the full load, and the count stands seven of the twelve days since the sixteenth, and the islands were apart, and at the omen's hour the Orchard's orchard stood straight and nothing leaned, and the omen was not logged, and the knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. On 08-28 the Garden-Bearer is not in the water, and the count stands seven of the thirteen days since the sixteenth, and the islands were apart, and at the omen's hour the Orchard's orchard stood straight and nothing leaned, and the omen was not logged, and the knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back. On 08-29 the Garden-Bearer is in the water with the full load, and the count stands eight of the fourteen days since the sixteenth, and the islands were apart, and at the omen's hour the Orchard's orchard stood straight and nothing leaned, and the omen was not logged, and the knot of twine is still on the two-inch branch, and the other two inches are not in the log, and neither is what the empty gap was leaning back.
  6. Fuel: four days of oil in the store. On 08-21 two days of oil left the store in one day and nothing was found. On 08-22 the store held at two days after burning one, which the arithmetic does not do; the store was counted twice. On 08-23 the jar omen was logged and the store held at two days, and the arithmetic still did not do. On 08-24 the store rose to three days after burning one, the opposite of not doing, and Wren counted it twice and it says three. On 08-25 the store rose to four days after burning one, and the gain is in the store, in the keeper's count, in front of the keeper. On 08-26 the store rose to four days after burning one, the third rise in a row, and the gain is in the store, in the keeper's count, in front of the keeper. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. On 08-27 the jar omen recurred and the store fell to two days after burning one, which the arithmetic does not do, after the three rises in a row on the twenty-fourth, the twenty-fifth, and the twenty-sixth; the store was counted twice, and the missing day is in the log without a name. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. On 08-28 the store held at two days after burning one, which the arithmetic does not do, the way it did on the twenty-second and the twenty-third, after the fall on the twenty-seventh; the store was counted twice, and the arithmetic still does not do. The jar was set empty on the table at night and was found empty at the third bell, and the jar did not fill, and the log does not say why the jar did not fill. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is. On 08-29 the store rose to five days after burning one, the biggest gain in this log, three days, after the hold on the twenty-eighth; the store was counted twice and it says five, and the arithmetic does not do. The jar was set empty on the table at night, the way it was set on the twenty-third and the twenty-eighth, and was found full at the third bell, the third filling in this log, and was poured into the store, and the gain is in the store, in the keeper's count, in front of the keeper, and the log does not say whether the jar is the gain or the gain is the jar. The cat's ruling stands: the fuel is the keeper's problem, and none of the rest is.
